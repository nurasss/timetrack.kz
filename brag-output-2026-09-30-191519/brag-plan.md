# Brag Plan: Timetrack.kz

## Planning rubric
1. App: tablet-based attendance with face recognition, liveness verification and automatic timesheets.
2. Strongest claim: «Автоматический учет рабочего времени без ручных табелей».
3. Visual hook: enormous «Без ручных табелей.» beside the site's real tablet photo.
4. Actual UI: tablet arrival control, blink confirmation, and the published demonstration timesheet.
5. Shortest satisfying film: 20 seconds.
6. Tone: polished; warm editorial product film with confident blue accents.
7. Audio: steady business bed and three quiet motion-matched cues, no narration.
8. Caption: Сделали Timetrack: отметка на планшете с подтверждением личности — и рабочее время уже в табеле. Без ручного заполнения. timetrack.kz
9. Flow: selfie → blink/head-turn confirmation → arrival → automatic timesheet.

## The angle
One check-in. No manual timesheet. Demonstrate the path rather than list features.
## Visual identity
Background #f7f5f1; device field #ebe7e2; text #1c1c1c; accent #2563eb; success #22c55e. Roboto-like source typography with bundled local TimetrackSans assets. Real source logos and tablet hero.
## Format / duration
Landscape, 1920×1080, 30 fps, 20 seconds.
## Audio direction / Music cue guidance
Bundled Happy Beats / Business Moves vol.12 by ende.app. Preset read: estimated 109.96 BPM. Optional arrival near 8.74 and timesheet reveal near 10.93. Bed at 0.28, 0.3s fade-in and 1.5s fade-out. Three sparse cues: tablet tap, sheet arrival, final brand. Audio-reactive treatment: none; calm settled product evidence is the priority. Never sacrifice reading time to beats.
## Storyboard
### 1 — Hook — 3.2s
«Без ручных табелей.» in large black type, blue kicker «УЧЕТ РАБОЧЕГО ВРЕМЕНИ», actual tablet hero on right. Hook settles by 0.5s. No interaction. Warm music, no cue. Clean cut.
### 2 — Check-in — 6s
Tablet on left. Three steps on right arrive in order: «Селфи», «Подтверждение личности», «Прибытие». All remain for at least 2s. Cursor contacts the real arrival button; matched compression and ripple adapted from the installed press-ripple primitive. Success badge «Отметка сохранена». One click cue. Clean cut.
### 3 — Result — 7s
Headline «Отметка есть. Табель готов.» Wide timesheet with first week and attendance summary adapted from the site's published June 2026 demonstration. Rows arrive in order; numeric entries populate. «Пример готового табеля для 1С» held below. Clearly labeled demonstration data. One soft sheet arrival cue. Clean cut.
### 4 — Close — 3.8s
Blue field, white real logo, «Рабочее время — под контролем.» and oversized timetrack.kz. Tiny tablet image anchors close. One soft brand cue and fade-out bed. Hold final state through end.
Total: 3.2 + 6 + 7 + 3.8 = 20s.
